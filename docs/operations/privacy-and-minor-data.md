# Privacy and Minor Data

- Collect only account, class, learning event, and project data required for teaching.
- Do not expose one student's projects, prompts, reports, or files to another student.
- Teacher reports are limited to assigned classes; student reports are limited to the student.
- Provider payloads must not contain unnecessary identity fields.
- Define retention and deletion windows with the school or organization before production launch.
- Obtain required guardian and school approvals before using the service with minors.
